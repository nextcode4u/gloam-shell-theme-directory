## Version 2 treatment

Updated layout, material coverage and fonts are generated by `tools/build_collection.py` and `tools/theme_finishing.py` in this repository. The latter also authors the controls and ambient accents. Fonts use Gloam's DSFONT03 converter, four grayscale coverage levels and independent body/heading faces. Original font notices remain in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`;
OFL source pins are in `config/theme-fonts.json`. Morrow retains its DejaVu pairing.

Material shapes and control silhouettes: `tools/theme_materials.py`.

Independent illustrated icon masters: `source/art/koi-icons.png`.
Full-image materials and individual focus skins: `tools/collection_surfaces.py`; no repeated patches.

- Body: AtkinsonHyperlegible-Regular, 12px; line height 12.
- Heading: Cinzel, 11px; line height 16.
