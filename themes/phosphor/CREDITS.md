# Phosphor

Original pixel artwork and synthesized audio by the Gloam Shell NDX project.
Rebuild with `python tools/make_theme_samples.py` using the pinned Pillow version.
These original theme assets may be used, modified and redistributed under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original icons in `icons/` are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0.
Reproduce them with `tools/make_theme_icons.py`; the artwork is authored for each visual family.

The modular UI images in `components/` are original CC0-1.0 artwork.
Their original versions were generated by `tools/make_theme_components.py`;
the current material skins and controls are authored by this directory’s
`tools/theme_materials.py`, under the same CC0-1.0 dedication.

Scene: Green radar station. Original CC0-1.0 pixel illustration by the Gloam project.
Reproduce with `tools/make_theme_scenes.py`; no third-party theme artwork is included.

Dedicated navigation icons and revised System/Settings gears are original CC0-1.0
artwork, reproduced by `tools/make_theme_navigation.py` and `tools/make_theme_icons.py`.

## Source

Imported from [Gloam Shell NDX 1.0.0](https://github.com/nextcode4u/gloam-shell-ndx/tree/v1.0.0/sdroot/_nds/dsi-frontend/themes/phosphor).
The generator paths above refer to that source repository, not this collection.
Theme configuration retains Gloam’s GPL-3.0-or-later terms; the original artwork
and synthesized audio retain their CC0-1.0 dedication.

## Version 2 treatment

Updated layout, material coverage and fonts are generated by `tools/build_collection.py` and `tools/theme_finishing.py` in this repository. The latter also authors the controls and ambient accents. Fonts use Gloam's DSFONT03 converter, four grayscale coverage levels and independent body/heading faces. Original font notices remain in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`;
OFL source pins are in `config/theme-fonts.json`. Morrow retains its DejaVu pairing.

Material shapes and control silhouettes: `tools/theme_materials.py`.

Independent illustrated icon masters: `source/art/phosphor-icons.png`.
Full-image materials and individual focus skins: `tools/collection_surfaces.py`; no repeated patches.

- Body: IBMPlexSansCondensed-Regular, 12px; line height 12.
- Heading: SpaceGrotesk, 14px; line height 16.
