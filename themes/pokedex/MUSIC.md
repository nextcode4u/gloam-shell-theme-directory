# Background music

“Comfortable Mystery 3” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com).

Source: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100536

Gentle electric piano at 65 BPM replaces the faster polka. Adapted into a
29.54-second circular loop, converted to mono PCM16 at 16 kHz, filtered and
loudness-normalized. This edit is not endorsed by the artist.
Full terms: MUSIC-LICENSE.txt. Reviewed source/output hashes and conversion
parameters are in config/theme-music.json. UI sounds are separately authored
project assets; no original Pokémon/Nintendo music is included.
